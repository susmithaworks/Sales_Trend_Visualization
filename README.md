WALMART SALES ANALYSIS DASHBOARD

Project Overview:
Developed an interactive Walmart Sales Dashboard using Power BI to analyze sales performance across stores, months, years, and holiday periods. The dashboard helps users monitor key performance indicators, identify sales trends, and make data-driven business decisions.

Key Features:
• Designed KPI cards for Total Sales, Average Weekly Sales, Average Fuel Price, and Average Temperature.
• Created interactive visualizations for Sales Trend Over Time, Sales by Year, Sales by Month, Sales by Store, and Sales by Holiday Flag.
• Implemented slicers for Date, Store, Year, Month, and Holiday Flag.
• Performed data cleaning and transformation using Power Query.
• Standardized date formats and handled data quality issues.

Business Insights:
• Identified seasonal sales patterns and peak revenue periods.
• Compared sales performance across multiple stores.
• Analyzed the impact of holidays on weekly sales.
• Evaluated the relationship between sales, temperature, and fuel prices.

Tools & Technologies:

Outcome:
Developed a professional business intelligence dashboard that provides actionable insights into Walmart sales performance and supports effective decision-making.
